Sample Offer Submitted

“Congratulations! You have been selected for a work-from-home internship. Pay ₹499 as a refundable registration fee to confirm your position.”
High Risk – Warning signs detected This example uses fictional sample text
  • Upfront payment requested (₹499).
  • Refundable fee language detected.
  • Company identity not independently verified.
Recommended actions: Do not transfer ₹499. Legitimate internships never require a refundable registration fee to confirm selection. Verify the employer independently via their official careers website and consult your college placement cell.
